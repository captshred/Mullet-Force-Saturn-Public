## Coordinated native assembly

The previous jab used an idle-derived head and frozen lower stance with the extended torso. Its roots/crops were registered consistently, but there was no coordinated weight transfer between those independently sourced regions. This pass changes native *piece assembly*, not character artwork: head and shoulder pieces move together, waist follows with a1px relative twist, hips move1px, knees shift1px towards the lead side, and both boots remain exactly fixed. There is no full-sprite regeneration, recolor, rescale, new palette or new texture. The exact original face, hair, jacket/vest, arm and leg pixels are reused. Neck bands share offsets; waist/thigh joins overlap instead of opening gaps, with explicit front ordering at overlapping rows.

Guard is the approved idle pose. Extension counters1–5 remain immediate; impact6–10 adds subtle body compression; recovery11–16 uses the approved guard with a1px forward body shift, then settles17–24. Buck's head/shoulders advance2px/down1px for extension and3px/down2px for impact; Chuck advances1px/down1px, then2px/down1px. Waist/hip motion is smaller; hips press down while knees move inward relative to grounded boots. Recovery head/body move together1px horizontally; feet remain fixed. The profiles are centralized in `src/jab_visual.h`. This is a compact native piece-motion refinement, not newly hand-drawn anatomical inbetweens; the resulting small shears/pose transitions need visual approval in the review build.

## Repeat input, independent from hit and visual timing

`src/jab_config.h`: six-tick single-slot input buffer, earliest restart at attack counter13 (**12 elapsed simulation ticks**,200ms at60Hz). A single jab still lasts24 ticks. Damage/active ticks6–10, masks, hitboxes, physics and damage values are unchanged. A fresh second press is queued during an existing grounded non-bat jab; it can survive up to6 advancing simulation ticks including capture. An input at counter8 can wait until13; an earlier input that expires before13 does nothing. At13 or later a valid pending edge starts the next jab immediately, resets that new jab's hit mask once, and restarts its visual sequence. No idle gap is inserted. Maximum deliberate empty-space cadence is5 jabs/second versus the previous2.5; hit-stop extends real-time intervals normally.

Holding A never fills the repeat queue. Release/repress edges are required, and multiple edges can occupy only one pending slot. Hit-stop captures fresh edges but freezes queue age/eligibility. Hurt, death, pause, disconnect, airborne/landing states, torch, bat and buffered B priority discard the queue. Bat gestures, dash classification, torch charge/held-A behavior, normal bat damage, running-bat KO, scoring and the original momentum slide remain unchanged. New state is two integers per player, appended after the old game ABI:16 work-RAM bytes. Existing hit-stop A/B buffering is retained; it cannot duplicate a consumed jab edge. Input/consume additions are explicitly delimited in game.c/game.h; stripping those additions reproduces the exact preceding approved source outside this feature.

## Audio and graphics budgets

**Audio is not regenerated.** Review builds require `MF_PRESERVE_AUDIO=1`; CI sets it for every job. `tools/check-preserved-audio.py` only verifies committed SEQ/TON/MAP payload hashes and sizes, then existing bytes are packaged. Existing PCM instruments/SFX, music source/configuration, routing and driver are unchanged. The generator is not invoked by these builds.

VDP1 remains **457,632 allocated /856 free /229 textures**; delta0 bytes/0 textures/0 palette bytes. All previous sprite banks remain intact, including the now-unplayed follow-through resources. There are no extra resident frames or per-frame uploads, I/O or heap allocation. VDP2/backdrops and the existing8-bank/4096-byte palette allocation are unchanged. The856-byte margin remains tight, so this assembly-based refinement deliberately avoids new textures. Additional repeat state is16 work-RAM bytes plus small constant metadata/code.
